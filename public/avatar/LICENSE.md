## Kai

`kai.vrm` is a fictional, locally modified prototype used to test the clean male avatar direction in the MVP.

`kai-smart.vrm` and `kai-weekend.vrm` are additional derivatives of the same local prototype. They retain the same author and permission metadata. The smart variant uses a navy and mist-blue commute palette; the weekend variant uses sage and oat colors with locally modeled crossbody accessories.

- Base asset: `AvatarSample_C.vrm`
- Base author: VRoid Project / pixiv Inc.
- Official model page: https://hub.vroid.com/en/characters/1248981995540129234/models/8640547963669442173
- Official sample-model conditions: https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/4402394424089-VRoidPreset-A-Z
- Embedded permissions: avatar use, modification, redistribution, and corporate/individual commercial use allowed; attribution not required
- Local modifications: short dark-brown hair palette, eye proportions and color, deep-teal and off-white jacket palette, charcoal trousers, off-white shoes, coral drawstring accent, and display metadata
- Outfit-variant modifications: packed clothing texture recolors, footwear palettes, and original simple accessory geometry authored locally in Blender
- Built with: Blender 4.5 LTS and VRM Add-on for Blender 4.5.0
- Recorded: 2026-08-12

The VRoid sample terms are permissive but are not a CC0 dedication. Do not sell the unmodified sample model, misrepresent it as an original model, or describe it as CC0. Review the current official conditions before distributing a production derivative.
